Should a fraud cutoff change every month?

I tested two alert policies on the same frozen model using synthetic card transactions from July through December 2020. One held the cutoff fixed. The other updated it monthly. Both had the same review allowance.

What changed when I counted reviews

The monthly policy caught 330 frauds and the fixed policy caught 302. But the first policy also sent 362 transactions for review, compared with 331 for the second. A shared allowance still left the reviewers with different amounts of work.

For each month, I took the smaller of the two review counts and compared the policies at that count. Across the six months, each then caught 301 frauds in 330 reviews. That’s the comparison I’d look at if the constraint is the number of cases a team can investigate.
