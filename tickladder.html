TickLadder

The tape updates one price at a time. A size of zero removes that price from the book. At update five, the highest bid is 12000 and there’s another bid two ticks below. Advance one update to see what replaces it.

Finding the next price

Each side has an array of quantities. A bit records whether each price is occupied. A second bitmap records which groups still contain a price. When the best bid goes away, the engine can skip the empty groups.

This design needs a fixed price range. I kept an ordered-map implementation as a reference and compared individual quotes across 300,000 random updates. A missing sequence number stops replay before another quote is published.
